03 / Services

What gets tiled.

Domestic and light commercial work across Newquay, St Ives, Falmouth, Penzance and the rest of Cornwall. Supply-and-fit or fit-only.

01.

Bathroom tiling

Ensuites, family bathrooms and wet rooms — walls, floors, showers, recessed niches, feature walls and bookmatched marble. Tanking and membrane systems done properly underneath.

02.

Kitchen tiling

Splashbacks, floors, upstands and the detail around worktops and units. Straight cuts, tight corners, silicone you could set a watch by.

03.

04 / About

The man behind the beard.

Dan is The Bearded Tiler — a tiling contractor based in Newquay, working across Cornwall. One trade, done properly: bathrooms, kitchens and floors, from first board to final grout.

The reviews say it better than we can. Clients come back years later — one barbershop floor in Newquay is still the talking point five years on, and the same client called Dan straight back for a new kitchen. Another had him tile an entire downstairs and five bathrooms on one Cornwall project.

Clean on site, quick to reply, and fussy about the last half-millimetre. That's the whole business plan.
